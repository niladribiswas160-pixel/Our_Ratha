Our Story

A Tradition Across Generations

For more than 75 years, Rathayatra has been an integral part of our family heritage. What began with the devotion of our ancestors has grown over the years into a five-day celebration. Today, our family continues to preserve this cherished tradition, keeping faith, family and community at its heart.
